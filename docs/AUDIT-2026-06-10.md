## Round 3 — operator's challenge round (suite: 94/94 + week-sim 50/50)

The operator challenged three round-2 design choices; each was settled with
evidence, not argument:

- **Research deadline is now SELF-TUNING** (was fixed 75 min — only 36% above
  the worst observed normal run, one slow Opus day from a false fire). The
  fund records its own research durations per depth (`cycle_stats`, last 8
  runs, per-candidate normalized, only non-timeout runs can teach it), sets
  the cutoff at 1.75× its worst normal run scaled to today's candidate count,
  clamped to [per-depth floor: fast 20 / standard 50 / full 100, cap 150].
  It can only fire ~2× past the worst day ever seen — true-anomaly territory.
- **Lesson expiry is now SOFT** (was hard-drop at 120d). Stale lessons render
  under a "STALE — unconfirmed, weigh with caution" header (5 most recent)
  instead of vanishing; the review is instructed to explicitly re-confirm,
  re-caveat, or drop each one. Knowledge is never silently destroyed or
  silently trusted.
- **Combined debate+judge VALIDATED by live A/B** (3 tickers × 2 paths × 2
  samples, 18 real Opus calls): 11/12 rating agreement, the single flip was
  in the OLD two-call path; combined was rating-stable on all 3 tickers,
  conviction within ±4 (no sleeve gate would flip), theses ~20% shorter but
  citing the same concrete desk evidence. Verdict: keep combined.

**Week-in-the-life simulator** shipped: `tests/test_week_sim.py` (50 checks,
offline, <5s) — 5 scripted trading days exercising buys, drift-guard skips,
hard stops -> bench, SELL exits, trailing-stop profit takes, breaker trips,
the learning review at 5 closes, rotation, the paper market-hours gate, AND
full live-mode plumbing against a mock Public client (pending orders, partial
fills + refunds, the 150s exit chase, mode-flip refusals, cross-mode guards).
Run it before any live-mode flip.

**Two real bugs the simulator exposed, both fixed:**
1. Same-tick trade loss — Windows datetime.now() advances in ~16ms ticks, so
   alpha + core closing one SELL verdict together shared a `closed_at` and
   the ledger dedupe dropped one trade. Every closed trade now carries a
   unique `trade_id` (legacy rows keep their old dedupe keys — no re-record).
2. Rotation breaker was dead config — `etf_rotation.day_breaker_pct` existed
   but the sleeve never consulted `entries_allowed`. A tripped breaker now
   DEFERS the whole rotation to the next cycle (never sell-then-sit-in-cash).

## Round 4 — cash deployment (operator: "doesn't hold cash / too much in market?")

Diagnosis: over-deployment was already structurally impossible (no leverage,
position/sector/correlation caps, breakers, kill switches, $100 buffer, live
buying-power verification). Under-deployment was UNMANAGED — alpha sat at
24.7% invested ($18.7k idle) because the PM judge hands out conservative
target weights and nothing re-deployed the residue. For a SPY-benchmarked
mandate, cash drag loses by default.

Shipped (suites: 103/103 + week-sim 50/50 = 153):
- **Cash equitization** (`config.CASH_EQUITIZATION`, alpha only): idle cash
  above a 70% invested floor parks in SPY up to a 90% target. The parking is
  exempt from per-name stops and committee SELLs (like the strategic core),
  never consumes a max_positions slot, is excluded from the correlation
  basis, and is sold automatically — conviction outranks parking — to fund
  real buys. SKIPPED whenever the sleeve is de-risking (soft tier, breaker,
  halt): in a drawdown, cash IS the position.
- **Deployment health check**: "deployment — alpha 89%, core 90%, calls 0%,
  etf 99%" on the dashboard; warns only if an equitization-mandated sleeve
  sits >15pts under its floor while NOT de-risking (mechanism failure).
- Applied live same day: alpha went 24.7% -> 89.0% invested (22 SPY parked).
- Deliberate non-targets: calls desk (premium dry powder IS the design),
  core blend (55% core floor already), rotation (BIL cash filter is a
  deliberate defensive signal, not drag).

## Round 6 — operator design challenge: sleeve coverage, caps, mandate-aware learning
(suites 116/116 + 50/50 = 166)

- **Calls desk joins re-underwriting** — held-call UNDERLYINGS are researched
  every cycle, and a SELL/STRONG_SELL verdict force-closes the call urgently
  (`run_calls_only` thesis-break exits). A call whose narrative died was
  bleeding theta until a price stop; the ladder manages price, not narrative.
- **ETF rotation deliberately stays mechanical** — its edge is a VALIDATED
  ruleset (27.4% CAGR backtest); committee meddling would unvalidate it. Its
  monthly rank re-check IS its re-underwriting; stops/kill switch protect it.
- **Caps are now asymmetric by design** (operator: "some sleeves not having
  caps may be better — or no?"): single names cap at 10% of firm; diversified
  ETFs at 25% (`MAX_FIRM_ETF_PCT`) — rotation's validated 33%-of-sleeve slugs
  are exposure, not concentration, and must not be strangled by a stock-sized
  cap. Rotation's own allocation stays uncapped per its mandate.
- **The learning loop is now mandate-aware** (operator: "does beating the SPY
  go into learning?" — it did NOT): every ledger trade now records
  `spy_ret_pct` over its own holding window + `excess_ret_pct`; stats()
  exposes a `vs_spy` block (beat rate, avg excess); the review prompt opens
  with a BENCHMARK SCOREBOARD (per-sleeve since-inception vs SPY) and explicit
  instructions that a profitable trade lagging SPY is a mandate loss. Bull
  markets can no longer teach false lessons.

## Round 7 — operator goal: learnable caps + "best it can be"
(suites 126/126 + 50/50 = 176)

- **The learning loop now sees how every cap RESULTS** — `learning.blocked_audit()`
  reads the journal's block/skip events (firm cap, sector, correlation,
  liquidity, drift, earnings, degraded) and measures each blocked entry's
  forward SPY-relative outcome (21-session window): per gate, n / avg excess /
  missed-winners / avoided-losers. Fed to every review as the CAPS AUDIT.
  Same-day blocks correctly report nothing until forward data exists.
- **Caps are LEARNABLE within hard bounds** — `config.CAP_BOUNDS` (firm name
  6-18%, firm ETF 15-40%, sector 25-50%, correlation 0.5-0.85, drift 2-6%);
  the review's new Job 2b proposes `cap_tuning` with audit evidence (n>=5 per
  gate); `learning._apply_cap_tuning` clamps + persists to state/tuning.json;
  every live gate reads `risk.tuned_cap()` instead of raw config. Tests prove
  the drift guard literally widens when the review tunes it.
- **"Best it can be" is now the review's stated mandate** — the prompt opens
  with an AMBITION MANDATE (beating SPY is the FLOOR; adequacy is failure)
  backed by `learning.regret_audit()`: committee BUYs the firm never owned,
  their forward SPY-relative returns, and the single biggest miss — the
  measured gap between the fund and the best version of itself.
- **Graceful reloads** — POST /api/restart journals and exits; the 5-min

## Round 8 — outage recovery (operator's PC lost internet; the safety had a hole)
(suites 132/132 + 50/50 = 182)

Diagnosis: `StartWhenAvailable=True` covered the PC being OFF, but with the
PC on and the network DOWN the cycle ran anyway, produced zero verdicts, and
stamped research "fresh" — so the >26h catch-up never fired and the trading
day was silently lost. Four layers shipped:

1. **Zero-verdict guard** (`cycle.run_cycle`): no verdicts -> research log NOT
   stamped (yesterday's preserved), `cycle_retry.needed` flagged, loud failure.
2. **Reconnect retry** (app health loop): catch-up now fires on the retry flag
   OR staleness — but only when `health.internet_up()` (TCP-53 probe, no HTTP)
   confirms connectivity, so it never relaunches into a dead network.
3. **EOD catch-up** (`health.eod_catchup`): a missed 4:30 postmarket wrap
   (dividends + official EOD record) books exactly once after reconnect,
   never before 16:35 ET, deduped via the "eod" date marker.
4. **Task Scheduler**: `RunOnlyIfNetworkAvailable=True` on Premarket / Firm
   Cycle / Postmarket (UAC-approved) — with StartWhenAvailable, Windows now
   defers those runs until connectivity exists. Keepalive and Exit Monitor
   intentionally untouched: they must run offline.

Also this round: monitors respect sessions (equity sweeps only during
regular+extended hours; the 1s calls loop idles outside 9:30-16:00) — no
more overnight API burn or after-hours `mark_missing` noise; heartbeats
continue so the watchdog stays calm. New "internet" line on the health panel.
Verified for the operator: SPY is DOWN -1.58% since inception (737.05 ->
725.43); the fund's -1.34% means +0.24 vs SPY — both negative, fund ahead.
