# Loch Raven Capital — Institutional Intelligence and Controlled Learning (design specification)
Status: DESIGN ONLY. No new publisher connectors, model training, self-modifying code, or automated strategy promotion are implemented by this document.

## Source registry and rights
Use explicit per-publisher configuration: publisher, canonical domain, official feed or API, discovery URL, access rights, permitted excerpt length, retention policy, cadence, robots/terms review date, and source-family lineage. Initial candidates: WSJ, CNN Business, Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, CNBC; Goldman Sachs Insights, JPMorgan Research, Morgan Stanley Insights, BlackRock Investment Institute, Vanguard research, Fidelity research; SEC, Federal Reserve, BEA, BLS, FRED and issuer IR. Do not imply that a paid research product is publicly accessible. Collect public metadata, titles, timestamps, canonical URLs and permitted abstracts; licensed full text only with explicit entitlement. Do not scrape around authentication, CAPTCHAs or paywalls.

**Where this lives today.** The registry lives in `firm/publisher_registry.py` on main since 11 October 2026, where `Publisher` carries a name, host, landing URL, desks and an access default of `metadata_only`, `allowed_discovery` accepts only a registered discovery URL, and `metadata` returns records that are unverified, not eligible for retrieval and flagged for a rights review. The free collectors on main use a fixed host allowlist instead, `HOSTS` in `firm/research_transport.py` (SEC, FRED, BLS, the Federal Reserve and Yahoo), and `validate_research` in `firm/research_provenance.py` refuses a source URL that is not HTTPS or that carries credentials. Licensed reports reach the desks only as files the operator drops into the gitignored `research_docs/inbox/`, distilled by `scan_and_distill` in `firm/street_research.py`. Permitted excerpt length, retention, cadence, terms review date and source family lineage per publisher are not implemented, phase 1.

## Source acquisition pipeline
Discover -> rights check -> canonicalize -> identify article/report version -> validate publication/observation dates -> extract claims and forecasts -> entity/ticker/industry linking -> reconcile against original filings and datasets -> record disagreement -> quality score -> deduplicate by canonical document and originating data lineage -> desk-specific evidence packet -> retrieval evaluation. Preserve raw-to-normalized lineage, publication and observed times separately, parser version, confidence reasons, and retraction/correction history. Treat publisher opinion, sell-side estimates and company disclosures as distinct claim types. Never turn forecasts into audited financial facts. Do not count syndication or FRED mirrors as independent confirmations. Quarantine unknown or expired rights, mismatched entities, stale evidence, and impossible timestamps.

**Where this lives today.** `collect` in `scripts/free_source_collection.py` drives `sec_records`, `fred_record` and `chart` in `scripts/refresh_research.py` and its own `bls_record` and `fed_records`, all through `SourceClient` in `firm/research_transport.py`, which refuses redirects and unlisted hosts and enforces request, time and size budgets. `validate_research` in `firm/research_provenance.py` checks publication, observation and effective times, the source URL, the content hash and ticker form, and `persist` in `scripts/refresh_research.py` keeps each changed version while unchanged content keeps its first observation time. `reconciliation` in `scripts/free_source_collection.py` marks BLS series and their FRED mirrors as not independent, and `chart` quarantines adjusted price jumps above 80 percent. `retrieve` and `desk_evidence` in `firm/research_rag.py` filter by time, entity and desk. Rights checks arrive with the registry in phase 1. Claim and forecast extraction, claim reconciliation against filings, parser versions and correction history are not implemented, phase 2.

## Five desk operating contracts
Each desk must output: timestamp, instrument, horizon, hypothesis, method, citations with page/section where available, assumptions, quantified scenarios, contradictory evidence, uncertainty, failure conditions, and abstention reason.
* Fundamental: reconcile SEC 10-K/10-Q financial statements, revenue segmentation, cash conversion, ROIC, invested capital, debt/leases, dilution; build explicit 3-statement forecast, DCF and peer comps; reconcile price target to current price and sensitivity to WACC/terminal growth. Earnings releases are provisional until reconciled to filings.
* Technical: adjusted corporate-action-safe daily bars, regime/trend, volume, volatility and liquidity, transaction costs, benchmark comparison; no lookahead from incomplete sessions; technicals never replace executable broker quotes.
* Sentiment: entity-resolved article/event clustering, primary-versus-secondary lineage, surprise versus consensus where legally available, corrections, earnings-guidance revisions, opposing coverage, publisher biases; abstain when only duplicated headlines exist.
* Macro: release calendars and vintage timestamps, inflation/jobs/growth, yield curves, credit spreads, liquidity, monetary policy, scenario transmission to each industry; FRED observations must not masquerade as historical real-time vintages.
* Quant: factor exposures, covariance shrinkage, costs/slippage, turnover, walk-forward splits, embargoed purged validation for overlapping labels, multiple-testing adjustment, drawdowns, calibration and capacity; report uncertainty intervals and reject unstable edges.
Portfolio manager: adjudicate desk disagreements, source conflicts and regime risk; enforce hard risk limits and no-trade gates independent of model text. No live order or model promotion without explicit authorization.

**Where this lives today.** The desks are `DESKS` in `firm/analysts.py`, each seeing only its slice of the packet through `_DESK_VIEWS` and its own sources through `desk_evidence`. `run_research` returns one report per desk in the `_DESK_SCHEMA` shape of score, conviction, summary, key points and risks, so the output contract above is not implemented, phase 3. At standard depth one model call, `_panel_prompt`, writes all five reports. Inputs come from `build_packet` and `compute_technicals` in `firm/data_engine.py`, `earnings_filings` in `firm/edgar.py`, `fundamentals` in `firm/fmp.py`, `partition` in `firm/news_quality.py` and `upcoming` in `firm/macro_calendar.py`. The portfolio manager is `run_debate_and_verdict` in `firm/debate.py`, and the hard limits no model text can loosen are in `firm/risk.py`, such as `entries_allowed`, `check_firm_sector_cap` and `firm_daily_breaker`. Three statement forecasts, DCF reconciliation and contradiction aware memos are phase 3, and purged validation and multiple testing adjustment are phase 4.

## RAFT and continuous improvement
Retrieval-augmented fine-tuning is a separate training process, not an automatic property of RAG. Build training examples only from licensed or internally authored evidence with precise citation spans, distractors, correct abstentions, verified calculations and contrary evidence. Split by publication time, entity and report family to prevent leakage. Maintain immutable decision journal: features available at decision time, forecast, sizing rationale, ex-ante confidence, outcome and risk-adjusted attribution. Generate error taxonomies: missing evidence, stale data, arithmetic, hallucinated citation, source disagreement, bad calibration, strategy error, execution slippage.
Nightly self-healing may repair ingestion/indexes, retry safe sources and rollback broken parsers. Weekly evaluation may propose prompt/retrieval changes; candidate changes run in shadow mode against locked baselines. Model fine-tuning requires dataset-rights review, held-out temporal testing, and manual approval for promotion. Never allow a model to rewrite its own tests, erase failures, loosen risk controls, or self-approve promotion.

**Where this lives today.** `firm/memory_finetune.py` exports RAFT style SFT, KTO and reward datasets from the graded memo archive with a time split and a leakage check, trains nothing, and sets `model_promotion_allowed` to False. `scripts/evaluate_research.py` exports grounding candidates with distractors and abstentions for review, with `promote_model` False. `run_debate_and_verdict` in `firm/debate.py` checks each committee verdict with `check_citations` and `check_numbers` in `firm/memory_guard.py` and appends its rating, conviction, sources and check status to the hash chained log of `audit_append`. That log is the nearest public piece of the decision journal but holds no features, sizing rationale or outcome, and `journal` in `firm/state.py` drops old lines as it rotates. The verdict ledger, the fund's immutable decision journal, lands in the private deployment. `prepare_cycle` in `firm/research_automation.py` keeps the last good corpus when a refresh fails, and `run_review` in `firm/learning.py` tunes caps only inside hard bounds. Error taxonomies and shadow runs are not implemented, phase 5.

## Promotion gates and metrics
Track permitted-source coverage, median discovery lag, freshness by desk, provenance completeness, correction/retraction propagation, entity resolution precision, citation faithfulness, unsupported-claim rate, abstention accuracy, retrieval recall@k and MRR, source-family diversity, data leakage checks, forecast Brier score/calibration, directional accuracy, benchmark-relative returns net of costs, drawdown, Sharpe uncertainty and turnover. Pre-register acceptance thresholds versus a frozen baseline. Require no regression on security, privacy, no-trade gates and data rights; promote only on statistically meaningful out-of-sample evidence. Keep full rollback, canary/shadow mode and immutable audit log.

**Where this lives today.** `evaluate` in `scripts/evaluate_research.py` computes hit rate at three, mean reciprocal rank at five and the future exclusion check on curated development probes, which are not a held out benchmark. Freshness by desk and corpus coverage come from `health` in `firm/research_automation.py` and `corpus_status` in `firm/research_rag.py`, served read only at `/api/memory/research/status`. Directional hit rate and mean excess return against SPY over 20 trading days come from `walk_forward` in `firm/memory_eval.py` and `stats` in `firm/memory_archive.py`. Sharpe and maximum drawdown exist only for mechanical strategy backtests, `_stats` in `firm/backtest.py`. Temporal leakage is checked by the split in `firm/memory_finetune.py` and by `tests/test_research_integrity.py`. `_calibrate_conviction` in `firm/debate.py` rescales conviction against its own trailing spread and does not measure calibration against outcomes. Brier score, outcome calibration, bootstrap intervals, shadow comparison and every promotion gate are not implemented, phase 5.

### Promotion thresholds and minimum samples
These are the fund's own numbers, pre registered here before any candidate is measured. The verdict ledger, the fund's immutable decision journal landing in the private deployment, is the source of every cohort.
* Minimum cohort. A verdict cohort needs at least 60 graded verdicts per desk and horizon before any calibration or directional figure is reported, and at least 200 before it can promote anything. For a hit rate near 50 percent the 95 percent interval would be about 12.7 points either side at 60 verdicts and about 6.9 points at 200 if the verdicts were independent binary observations, which they are not, because 20 trading day horizons overlap and verdicts on related names move together, so the real intervals are wider and these figures only explain why reporting starts at 60 and promotion waits for 200, never the interval a report may quote.
* Brier score. A candidate must improve on the frozen baseline Brier score by at least 0.01, lower being better, measured paired, the candidate and the baseline scored on the same verdicts over the same windows, with a 95 percent interval on the paired improvement from a block or stationary bootstrap that resamples runs of consecutive sessions and so keeps the temporal dependence of overlapping horizons, and that interval must exclude zero. A forecaster that always says 50 percent scores 0.25, so 0.01 is 4 percent of that no skill score, and the interval guards against a lucky sample.
* Direction and excess return. Directional accuracy and excess return against SPY are reported with intervals and never promote on their own, because a few large moves or one regime can carry them while the forecasts stay poorly calibrated.
* Risk adjusted claims. No Sharpe or drawdown claim from fewer than 250 trading days, about one year of sessions and four earnings seasons, because a shorter window measures one stretch of one market.
* Shadow period. A candidate runs in shadow against the locked baseline until at least 200 of its own shadow verdicts have matured 20 trading day outcomes, the same cohort floor as above, and never for less than 20 trading days, one full turn of the horizon the fund already grades, `excess_20d` in `firm/memory_archive.py`. Twenty trading days alone yields only the first matured outcomes and never a cohort, so at the fund's verdict rate a shadow period runs for months.
* Decision. Every promotion is a manual decision by Patrick recorded in the decision journal, so no model approves itself and each promotion can be traced and reversed.

Nothing enforces these numbers yet, phase 5. The public repository documents them and the private deployment will apply them.

## Delivery phases
1. Publisher rights/registry and official RSS/API discovery, metadata-only ingestion.
2. Claim-level provenance, entity/industry routing, report corrections, freshness dashboards and source audits.
3. Desk operating contracts and contradiction-aware research memos with deterministic math verification.
4. Locked temporal retrieval, grounding and financial backtests.
5. RAFT candidate creation and shadow evaluation; manual model promotion after measured improvements.

**Where this lives today.** Phase 1 has its registry module on main, and official feed discovery runs on main in `fed_records` for the Federal Reserve feeds and in `bls_record` and `cik_lookup`, all in `scripts/free_source_collection.py`. Phase 2 is partial, with freshness by desk in `health` in `firm/research_automation.py` and the `lineage_group`, `source_tier` and `quality_warnings` fields the collectors write, and nothing yet for claim level provenance or corrections. Phase 3 is partial, with the desks in `firm/analysts.py`, the judge in `firm/debate.py` and deterministic figure tracing by `check_numbers` in `firm/memory_guard.py` on every committee verdict. Phase 4 is partial, with point in time retrieval in `firm/research_rag.py` and `firm/memory_rag.py` and mechanical backtests in `firm/backtest.py`, `scripts/sim_10y.py` and `scripts/sim_2025.py`. Phase 5 has dataset export only, in `firm/memory_finetune.py`, with no shadow evaluation and promotion disabled.

## Explicit limits
No performance or profit guarantee. No licensed Goldman Sachs/WSJ/Bloomberg full-text entitlement is assumed. Free public research varies by publisher. This specification alone does not activate feeds, train models, or change trading behavior.

**Where this lives today.** Paper trading is the default, and `update_settings` in `app.py` refuses a mode switch while any position, pending order or queued entry is open (lines 298 to 316). `DEMO_MODE` in `config.py` blanks the broker credentials. ### Measured Public SDK limits
Only figures the public code states or records as measured appear here, each with its file and line. Everything else is marked unverified, requires an entitlement test.
* Package. `publicdotcom-py>=0.1.10` (`requirements.txt` line 14), imported as `public_api_sdk`. `get_client` builds one client from `ApiKeyAuthConfig` and `PublicApiClientConfiguration` (`firm/broker.py` lines 33 to 42).
* Timeouts and retries. The SDK ships a 30 second timeout, 3 retries and a 0.3 backoff, `PublicApiClientConfiguration` exposes neither, and its HTTP adapter retries only HEAD, GET and OPTIONS, so POST retries run in process (`firm/broker.py` lines 43 to 52, `config.py` lines 1013 to 1017). The adapter sets 6 seconds per attempt, 1 retry and a 0.1 backoff on the client instance (`firm/broker.py` lines 55 to 63, `config.py` lines 1018 to 1019).
* Cold start. Measured on 28 August 2026 at 4.4 to 8.9 seconds to import the SDK plus about 1.0 second to construct and authenticate, 5.4 to 10.0 seconds in total, against about 45 ms per quote afterwards (`app.py` lines 1229 to 1233).
* Token. The access token lives 600 seconds and the SDK mints a new one on the next request. A bare mint measured 32 ms, and a quote that paid for one took 80 ms against 42 ms warm (`app.py` lines 1246 to 1248).
* Batch quotes. `get_quotes` takes a list of equity or option instruments (`firm/broker.py` lines 221 to 231 and 380 to 390), and a batch of 21 symbols measured 50 ms on 28 August 2026 against 8,239 ms for the yfinance fallback (`config.py` lines 37 to 39).
* Instruments. `get_all_instruments` filtered to EQUITY returned 22,890 instruments on 16 July 2026, each with its trading permission and its fractional and options flags. The unfiltered response model fails on BOND rows, so the fetch is always filtered to EQUITY, which at Public includes ETFs (`firm/tradable.py` lines 8 to 13).
* Orders and statuses. The adapter sends LIMIT or MARKET orders with DAY time in force, a CORE or EXTENDED session for equities and an open or close indicator for options (`firm/broker.py` lines 649 to 661), polls them with `get_order` and cancels stale ones with `cancel_order` (lines 1223 and 1272). The installed SDK maps any status it does not recognize to UNKNOWN (`firm/broker.py` lines 1196 to 1197).
* Research adapter. The adapter proposed on the closed PR 5 branch, not merged, marked premium analyst reports and premium fundamentals unverified, the options chain as requiring an SDK entitlement test and order permission as not used, and read only `live_account`, which calls `get_portfolio` (`firm/broker.py` lines 69 to 84).

### One host per job and private data
* The VPS private deployment is the only collector and the only trader.
* The public repository is a reviewed projection and never runs a scheduled job that commits data.
* No credential, no account number, no paper or live book, no position, no order, no personal path and no personal address appears in a public commit.
* A public pull request reaches the fund only after an inbound replay and review in the private deployment.
